Before you start

Does the demo visit any sites?

All sample records are fictional. Searching and watching them changes only the demo in this tab. Reloading resets the demo.

Do I need an account?

The research workspace requires a verified account. Account registration is managed by the operator. You can explore this demo without signing in.

What information does the service hold?

The workspace stores account information and your crawl records. You can choose whether new crawls retain page text, review retention settings and download your account data. The clearnet domain uses Cloudflare to process HTTPS connections.
